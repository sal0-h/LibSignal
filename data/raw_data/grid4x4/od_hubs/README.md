# Hub-centric OD demand (grid4x4)

Synthetic demand layer for LibSignal realism experiments (1800 s episodes).

- `taz.xml` — fringe, within-network (internal), and hub CBD zones
- `demand_set/` — generated by `extras/gen_od_hub_demand_grid4x4.py`

Hubs are inherent to the demand model (majority of trips touch `hub_cbd`).
Origins mix ~60–70% fringe and ~30–40% internal approach edges.
Routing: SUMO `duarouter` shortest path. See `docs/OD_HUB_DEMAND.md`.
